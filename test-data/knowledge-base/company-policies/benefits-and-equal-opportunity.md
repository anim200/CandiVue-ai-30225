# Benefits and Equal Opportunity

**Example Freight Ltd. (fictional company, synthetic test document)**

## What we offer every employee

- Two festival bonuses a year
- Health insurance for the employee, spouse and up to two children
- 20 days of annual leave plus public holidays
- A yearly learning budget of BDT 60,000 for courses, books and certifications
  (for example AWS or Zend certification exams)
- Paid time to take one certification exam a year
- Lunch on office days
- A provident fund with a 10% company contribution after confirmation

## Probation and growth

Probation is three months. Engineers have a growth review every six months
with their lead, covering technical skills, delivery and teamwork. Promotions
are decided on those reviews, not on years of service alone.

## Equal opportunity statement

Example Freight Ltd. hires on skills and experience only. We welcome applicants
of every background, gender, age, religion, ethnicity, nationality and marital
status, and people with disabilities. We do not ask for photos, date of birth
or family details in applications. Reasonable adjustments for interviews are
available on request: write to the hiring team at careers@example.com.

## How we interview

Interviews are a 45-minute video call with one interviewer. Candidates can ask
for the questions in writing if they prefer. Every interview is followed by a
written assessment that is reviewed by a person before any decision.
