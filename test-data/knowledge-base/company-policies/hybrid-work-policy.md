# Hybrid Work Policy

**Example Freight Ltd. (fictional company, synthetic test document)**
Version 3 · Applies to all engineering roles

## Where we work

Engineering teams work in a hybrid model: three days a week in the Dhaka office
(Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday) and two days from home. Team rituals such as
sprint planning, design reviews and retrospectives happen on office days so that
everyone can join in person.

New joiners spend their first four weeks fully in the office. This gives them
time to meet the team, pair with a buddy and learn our deployment process before
they start working remotely.

## Core hours

Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 Bangladesh time. Outside core hours, people plan
their own day. Meetings are not booked before 10:00 or after 17:00.

## Equipment

Every engineer receives a laptop (16 GB RAM or more), a second monitor for home
and a monthly internet allowance of BDT 2,000. Docker Desktop and the company
VPN are installed on day one.

## On-call

Backend engineers join the on-call rotation after six months. On-call is one
week in eight, paid with a fixed weekly allowance plus time off in lieu for any
night call-out.

## Travel

Some roles visit the warehouse sites in Chattogram twice a year. Travel and
accommodation are booked and paid by the company.
